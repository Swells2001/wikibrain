Step 2 — Tell Us About Your Passions
These are my passions because they are what I have found throughout my life that I
enjoy and make me happy. I feel a sense of accomplishment and fulfillment within my
heart when doing them. They interest and inspire me to become better at certain tasks
and learn more about them. There are things I wish I could do but don't know how to yet.
I wish I could program anything I wanted or do a backflip. I wish I was better at
speaking to big crowds and reading contracts for athletes. I wish I could read the stock
market better and have a better understanding of valuations and how to leverage
different stocks.

Step 3 — Find Your Starting Point: What the World NEEDS
A few needs claude thought I could address were:
- Youth/adolescent hockey players need better, more patient skill development. Sport
  passion + leadership + adaptability to run drills, clinics, or 1-on-1 lessons beyond
  what overworked rec-league coaches offer.
- Amateur and semi-pro athletes need someone they trust to handle deals and
  negotiations. Fits sports agent interest + networking skill, points at the contract
  law improvement goal.
- Small businesses/startups need pipeline and client relationships built. Sales
  interest + networking strength + critical thinking.
- Local running/cycling communities need organizers for group rides, races, or training
  plans. Detail-oriented + observant + genuine passion for the sport.
- Firefighter candidates need structured physical/mental readiness coaching. Firefighting
  interest + leadership + adaptability.
- Teams or athletes need someone who can read data, not just plays. AI/program skills +
  detail-oriented nature -> sports performance/recruiting analysis.
- Community groups need engaging storytellers for local history. History passion +
  public-speaking improvement goal (libraries, museums, youth programs).
