# Expert in the Loop

## Core
"Expert in the loop" is the idea that working well with AI requires domain expertise,
not just a person approving outputs ("human in the loop"). AI builds what you ask for
in the moment but doesn't reason about a project's direction, so an uninformed operator
accumulates fragile work.

## Key Points
- **Greenfield effect:** piling AI-generated features on a weak foundation eventually
  breaks; plan and learn fundamentals first, and don't accept answers you don't
  understand.
- **Curiosity beats resistance:** knowing what AI can do is increasingly expected;
  avoiding it entirely is the bigger risk.
- **Guard creativity:** because AI makes decent output easy, personal input (your own
  ideas/lyrics first) is what makes the result feel like yours.
- **Dueling models:** a buildable-in-a-day setup running work through two
  oppositely-tuned models (critical vs positive) to surface disagreement and get a
  genuine "second opinion."

## Details
These ideas come from [[Noah]]'s talk as recorded in the ITM 370 Week 9 notes. The
dueling-models "second opinion" idea rhymes with the contradiction-flagging goal of the
[[Persistent Wiki]] pattern — both try to keep AI from simply affirming whatever you
already believe.
