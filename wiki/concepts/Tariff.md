# Tariff

## Core
A tariff is a tax on imported goods. It is the main contrast case for an
[[Import Quota]]: both restrict imports and raise domestic prices, but a tariff
generates government revenue rather than [[Quota Rent]].

## Key Points
- Creates government revenue directly from the tax on imports.
- A tariff-rate quota (TRQ) blends the two: a low tariff up to a threshold, higher
  beyond it.
