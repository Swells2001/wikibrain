# Market Gap Analysis

## Core
Market gap analysis compares competitors on what they do well and where they're weak to
find an unserved opening. In the project it exposed the gap that defines
[[Youth Hockey Skills Training]].

## Key Points
- The Boise market splits into expensive in-person (private lessons, camps) and cheap
  online (no feedback).
- Open middle: affordable small-group coaching with a trusted coach and regular parent
  progress updates.
- Competitors mapped: [[Idaho IceWorld]], [[Next Level Hockey Idaho]], and others.
