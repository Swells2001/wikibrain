# Customer Service

## Core
Customer service is the skill of helping people get what they came for and leave
satisfied. At [[Quail Hollow Golf Course]] it shows up in every interaction with
golfers booking and playing.

## Key Points
- Front-line, in-person and over the phone.
- Underpins repeat business and reputation.
- Closely tied to [[Conflict Resolution]] when expectations aren't met.
