# Quota Rent

## Core
Quota rent is the profit earned from being allowed to import a good under an
[[Import Quota]]. Because the quota pushes the domestic price above the world price,
whoever holds the right to import at the world price captures the difference.

## Key Points
- Arises specifically under a quota, not a [[Tariff]] (which yields government revenue
  instead).
- Who captures it depends on license allocation.
- If import licenses are auctioned, the government collects the rent as revenue.
