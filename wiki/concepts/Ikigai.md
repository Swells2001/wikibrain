# Ikigai

## Core
Ikigai is a framework for finding direction at the intersection of what you love, what
you're good at, what you can be paid for, and what the world needs. Here it's the
structured exercise that produced [[Youth Hockey Skills Training]].

## Key Points
- Part 1 (Discover): inventory passions, work interests, and skills; find needs you
  could meet.
- Part 2 (Opportunity): research competitors, talk to a customer, stress-test, decide.
- Part 3 (Validate): build a validated brief *(pending)*.
