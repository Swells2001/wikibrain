# CRM Skills

## Core
CRM (customer relationship management) skills are the ability to use software that
tracks customers, bookings, and history. At [[Quail Hollow Golf Course]] this covers
the tee-sheet/booking system and member records.

## Key Points
- Managing bookings, guest records, and history in software.
- Turns repeat interactions into data that supports [[Sales Skills]].
