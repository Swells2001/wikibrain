# Small Open Economy

## Core
A small open economy is a country small enough relative to the world market that it
takes the world price Pw as given. It is the standard setting for analyzing how an
[[Import Quota]] affects domestic prices and imports.

## Key Points
- Takes the world price Pw as a given; cannot influence it.
- Under free trade the domestic price equals the world price: Pd = Pw.
- At Pw, domestic firms supply Q1 and consumers demand Q2; since Q2 > Q1 the country
  imports M = Q2 − Q1.
- Imposing a quota raises the domestic price to Pq until supply rises, demand falls,
  and imports equal the cap.
