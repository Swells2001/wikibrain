# YAML Frontmatter

## Core
YAML frontmatter is the structured metadata block at the top of a [[Markdown]] file.
The [[Open Knowledge Format]] uses it to hold the small set of queryable fields (type,
resource, tags, generated, status) while the body carries prose and schemas.

## Key Points
- Separates queryable/filterable structured data from human-readable body content.
- Lets markdown files be indexed, filtered, and trust-signaled without a database.
