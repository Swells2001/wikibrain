# Sales Skills

## Core
Sales skills are the ability to promote and close — upselling tee times, memberships,
and services. Built at [[Quail Hollow Golf Course]] through guest and member
interactions.

## Key Points
- Upselling and promoting services during bookings.
- Builds on [[Customer Service]] and [[CRM Skills]] (knowing the customer).
- Measured by conversions and repeat business.
