# AI Stress Test

## Core
An AI stress test uses an AI as a devil's advocate to challenge an idea before
committing. Solomon used Claude to pressure-test [[Youth Hockey Skills Training]].

## Key Points
- Surfaced risks: scarce ice access, tight unit economics, middleman risk, trust/
  vetting gaps, thin evidence, copycat competitors.
- Forced sharper differentiators: affordable price, a trusted coach, and parent
  progress updates (not vague "personal experience").
- A real-world instance of the [[Expert in the Loop]] "dueling models" idea — using AI
  to push back rather than just affirm.
