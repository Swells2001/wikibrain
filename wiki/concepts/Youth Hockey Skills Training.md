# Youth Hockey Skills Training

## Core
The project [[Solomon Wells]] chose to build: small-group (4–6 kids) hockey skills
training with monthly parent progress reports, for Boise players ages 8–12. It targets
the gap between expensive private lessons/camps and feedback-free online video.

## Key Points
- Format: recurring small groups of 4–6, ages 8–12, in Boise.
- Price: ~$25–$35 per player per session, with a monthly package option.
- Differentiators (from [[Customer Discovery]]): affordable price, a coach parents
  trust, and a short monthly written progress update.
- Venue idea: off-peak rink ice or synthetic ice (e.g. [[Next Level Hockey Idaho]]) to
  keep costs down.

## Details
Emerged from [[Ikigai]] Part 1 as the most buildable need, then validated in Part 2 via
[[Market Gap Analysis]], a [[Customer Discovery]] call with [[Robin]], and an
[[AI Stress Test]]. Chosen over athlete contract support and sports performance/
recruiting analysis. Main risks: affordable ice access and unit economics
(illustrative break-even ~$120/session → 4 kids @ $30 breaks even). Open items tracked
in [[My Project]].
