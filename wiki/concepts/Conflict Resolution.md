# Conflict Resolution

## Core
Conflict resolution is the skill of defusing disagreements and finding a workable
outcome. Running the tee sheet at [[Quail Hollow Golf Course]] surfaces it constantly —
double-bookings, pace-of-play disputes, and scheduling clashes.

## Key Points
- Needed when the schedule and reality collide (overlaps, delays, upset guests).
- Pairs with [[Customer Service]]: resolving the problem while keeping the guest happy.
- Often handled live over the phone (see [[Phone Skills]]).
