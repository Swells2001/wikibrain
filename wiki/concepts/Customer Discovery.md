# Customer Discovery

## Core
Customer discovery is talking to a real potential customer to test a problem and idea
before building. The [[Robin]] interview did this for
[[Youth Hockey Skills Training]].

## Key Points
- Surfaced the real price band (~$25–$35/session) and a preference for monthly packages.
- Confirmed the problem: team practice doesn't give individual attention.
- Key insight that reshaped the plan: a good player isn't automatically a good coach —
  trust and teaching ability lead.
- Treat a friendly interviewee's interest as encouraging, not proof.
