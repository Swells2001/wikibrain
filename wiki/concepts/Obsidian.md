# Obsidian

## Core
Obsidian is a markdown-based knowledge management app with a graph view. In this vault
it is the browsing/consumption front-end for both the [[Open Knowledge Format]] and the
[[Persistent Wiki]] pattern.

## Key Points
- Reads plain [[Markdown]] files directly, so OKF bundles and llm-wikis need no custom
  UI.
- Draws graph edges from `[[wikilinks]]` in the note body.
- In the llm-wiki pattern: "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is
  the codebase."
