# Phone Skills

## Core
Phone skills are the ability to communicate clearly and professionally by phone —
taking bookings, answering questions, and handling issues without visual cues. Core to
running the tee sheet at [[Quail Hollow Golf Course]].

## Key Points
- Primary channel for tee-time bookings and inquiries.
- Carries [[Customer Service]] and [[Conflict Resolution]] over voice alone.
- Feeds booking data into [[CRM Skills]].
