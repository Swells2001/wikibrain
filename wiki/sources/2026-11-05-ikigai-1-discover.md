# Ikigai Part 1 — Discover

Authored 2026-09-02 by [[Solomon Wells]]. The discovery step of the [[Ikigai]]
exercise: inventorying passions, work interests, skills, and the needs they could meet.

## Step 1 — Inventory (four lists)
**Personal interests / passions:** Ice Hockey, Music, History, Bicycles, Running.
**Professional interests (work):** Sales, Ice Hockey Coach, Sports Agent, Firefighting.
**Skills I'm confident in:** networking and connecting with others, [[Leadership]],
adaptability and critical thinking, being a detailed and observant worker.
**Skills I want to improve:** [[Public Speaking]], stock and property valuation,
contract law, AI/programming skills.

## Step 2 — About the passions
These are my passions because they're what I've found over my life that I enjoy and
that give me a sense of accomplishment and fulfillment; they inspire me to improve and
learn more. Things I wish I could do but can't yet: program anything I want, do a
backflip, speak to big crowds, read athlete contracts, and read the stock market /
valuations better. Those "possible passions" map onto the skills-to-improve list.

## Step 3 — What the world NEEDS
Fed the Step 1 brainstorm into a chatbot to generate needs that fit me. Candidates:
- Youth/adolescent hockey players need patient, individual skill development
  (passion + [[Leadership]] + adaptability). -> leads to [[Youth Hockey Skills Training]].
- Amateur/semi-pro athletes need trusted deal/negotiation help (sports-agent interest +
  networking; points at the contract-law goal).
- Small businesses/startups need pipeline and client relationships built
  ([[Sales Skills]] + networking + critical thinking).
- Local running/cycling communities need organizers (detail-oriented + observant +
  passion for the sport).
- Firefighter candidates need structured physical/mental readiness coaching.
- Teams/athletes need people who can read data, not just plays (AI/programming goal +
  detail-oriented -> sports performance/recruiting analysis).
- Community groups need engaging storytellers for local history (history passion +
  [[Public Speaking]] goal).
