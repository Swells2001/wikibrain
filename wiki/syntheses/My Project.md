# My Project

Map-of-Content for [[Solomon Wells]]'s Ikigai project: building
[[Youth Hockey Skills Training]] — small-group (4–6 kids) hockey skills coaching with
monthly parent progress reports, for Boise players ages 8–12.

## The three Ikigai parts
1. [[Ikigai Part 1 — Discover]] — passions, skills, and the needs they could meet.
2. [[Ikigai Part 2 — Find Your Opportunity]] — competitor research, customer interview,
   AI stress test, and the decision.
3. [[Ikigai Part 3 — Validate]] — Validated Build Brief *(pending)*.

## The decision
Build small-group hockey skills training (4–6 kids, ages 8–12, Boise) with monthly
parent progress updates. Differentiators: affordable price (~$25–$35/session), a coach
parents trust, and regular progress communication.
