# Idaho IceWorld

## Core
Idaho IceWorld is a Boise rink and, via Boise Skills Academy (BSA), the main incumbent
competitor to [[Youth Hockey Skills Training]]. It has an exclusive City of Boise
partnership.

## Key Points
- Offers Learn to Play, house league, and year-round clinics, scrimmages, and camps
  (also hosts the Mylymok camps, $275/player).
- Strengths: clear development pathway, strong local trust, city partnership.
- Weaknesses for the gap: only two rinks (ice fills fast, crowded sessions); BSA clinic
  prices not public. Could copy a small-group offering.
