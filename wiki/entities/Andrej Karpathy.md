# Andrej Karpathy

## Core
Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and engineer. In this vault he appears as the
author of the llm-wiki gist that articulates the [[Persistent Wiki]] pattern.

## Key Points
- Authored the llm-wiki "idea file" describing LLM-maintained knowledge bases.
- Frames the approach in contrast to [[RAG]] and describes an [[Obsidian]]-plus-agent
  working setup.

## Details
In the gist, Karpathy describes working with an LLM agent on one side and Obsidian on
the other, browsing the wiki in real time while the agent makes edits — summarized in
the line "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase."
