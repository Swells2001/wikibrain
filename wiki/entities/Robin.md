# Robin

## Core
Robin is a Boise hockey parent (10-year-old competitive player) interviewed by
[[Solomon Wells]] on 2026-10-28 for the [[Youth Hockey Skills Training]] project. Her
feedback reshaped the plan.

## Key Points
- Team practices don't give enough individual attention; private lessons ($50–$80) and
  camps feel hard to justify.
- Likes 4–6 kid groups; would "definitely consider" at ~$25–$35/session; prefers a
  monthly package; wants simple monthly progress updates.
- Key insight: "being a good hockey player doesn't automatically mean you're a good
  coach" — trust and teaching ability matter most. Reviews from other parents sway her.
