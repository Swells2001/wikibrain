# Quail Hollow Golf Course

## Core
Quail Hollow Golf Course is a golf course and the workplace this page tracks. The role
here centers on running the tee sheet and keeping the course operating smoothly day to
day.

## Key Points
- Role: run the **tee sheet** — scheduling and sequencing tee times so play flows.
- Responsibility: keep the course running smoothly (pace of play, bookings, guest
  flow).
- Builds a cluster of customer-facing and sales skills (see Related).

## Details
Operating the tee sheet means managing bookings, spacing groups to avoid backups, and
handling the inevitable conflicts when the schedule and reality collide. The job
exercises front-line [[Customer Service]] and [[Conflict Resolution]], heavy
[[Phone Skills]], and — through booking and membership systems — [[CRM Skills]] and
[[Sales Skills]].
