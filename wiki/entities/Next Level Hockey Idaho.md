# Next Level Hockey Idaho

## Core
Next Level Hockey Idaho is a 2,000 sq ft indoor synthetic-ice training space in Garden
City — a competitor to, and a possible facility option for,
[[Youth Hockey Skills Training]]. Contact noted: Jackson Howell.

## Key Points
- Formats: individual, semi-private, goalie, shooter, beginner, elite.
- Strength: flexible formats in a dedicated space not dependent on scarce rink ice.
- Weakness: brand new, little name recognition; no public pricing/schedule found.
- Relevant because synthetic ice is a candidate low-cost venue for the project.
