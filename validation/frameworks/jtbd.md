# Framework 4: Jobs To Be Done

Run Jobs To Be Done on the project in `validation/project.md`.

## Output format

1. **Job statement** in the form: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]."
2. **The three jobs:** functional, emotional, and social.
3. **Hired today:** what the user "hires" today instead, and why they might "fire" it.
4. **Three interview questions** about past behavior.

## Rules

- The job must come from what a real person said or did, not from my solution. If there is no evidence yet, write the job as a hypothesis and say so.
- No interview question may ask whether the person would use my idea. Ask about what they have already done, paid for, or stopped doing.
- Cite evidence from `project.md` for every point, or say "no evidence yet".
- Tag each point as [real person], [desk research], [AI opinion], or [no evidence yet].
- Keep the whole output under 300 words.
