# Framework 3: SWOT

Run a SWOT analysis on the project in `validation/project.md`.

## Inside and outside

- **Strengths** and **Weaknesses** are about me and the project (inside).
- **Opportunities** and **Threats** are about the outside world.

Do not put an outside factor in an inside box, or the reverse.

## Output format

A 2x2 Markdown table with at most 3 items per box:

| | Helpful | Harmful |
|---|---|---|
| **Inside** | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| **Outside** | Opportunities | Threats |

Then a final list called `Moves`.

## Rules

- Every item names its evidence or says "no evidence yet".
- Each Weakness and each Threat is paired with one action in the `Moves` list.
- Tag each item as [real person], [desk research], [AI opinion], or [no evidence yet].
- Keep the whole output under 300 words.
