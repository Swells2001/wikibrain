# Framework 1: Six Thinking Hats

Run Edward de Bono's Six Thinking Hats on the project in `validation/project.md`.

## Question for each hat

- **White (facts):** What do we actually know, and what is missing?
- **Red (feelings):** How would users feel, and how do I feel? No justification needed.
- **Black (risks):** What could make this fail, and why?
- **Yellow (benefits):** What are the benefits, and why could it work?
- **Green (new ideas):** What alternatives or new ideas are there?
- **Blue (process):** What is the one next step?

## Output format

- One Markdown section per hat, in the order above.
- 2 to 4 bullets per hat.
- A final line: `Decision it supports:`

## Rules

- The Black hat must name at least 2 real risks that are specific to this project, not generic ones such as "competition" or "it might not work".
- Cite evidence from `project.md` for every bullet, or say "no evidence yet".
- Tag each bullet as [real person], [desk research], [AI opinion], or [no evidence yet].
- Keep the whole output under 300 words.
